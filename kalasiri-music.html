Our Story

A Sanctuary for Musical Souls

Kalasiri School Of Music is a premier destination for hobby enthusiasts in Kuvempunagar, Tumkur. With passionate instructors who are experts in their fields, we provide a supportive, friendly learning environment that encourages creativity and exploration.

Our mission is to inspire creativity and lifelong learning through engaging, hands-on music experiences. We believe music enhances well-being, fosters community, and ignites personal growth.
