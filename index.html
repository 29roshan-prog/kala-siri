Our Story

A Home for Every Musical Soul

Kalasiri School of Music has been nurturing musical talent for over 12 years. Founded with a passion for Indian classical arts, we offer structured music education in a warm, encouraging environment that celebrates every student's journey.

We offer music education across a wide range of styles, bringing together tradition, devotion, and joy of music — from the youngest learners to housewives discovering their musical side.
